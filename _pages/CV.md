## Research areas

- Numerical analysis and structure-preserving discretization of partial differential equations
- Well-posedness, regularity, stability, and long-time behaviour
- Fractional, nonlocal, and stochastic PDEs
- Feedback control and stabilization
- Multiphysics, mixed-dimensional, and mathematical life-science models
