My research combines analytical questions—existence, uniqueness, regularity, stability,
and long-time behaviour—with numerical methods designed to respect the mathematical
structure of the model.

## Structure-preserving discretization

Many evolution equations encode conservation laws, energy dissipation, positivity, or
thermodynamic consistency. I study finite-element and time-stepping methods that retain
these properties at the discrete level, particularly for Cahn–Hilliard-type systems,
degenerate mobilities, and coupled multiphysics models.

Representative work:

- [High-order conforming finite elements for the Cahn–Hilliard equation]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}#pub30)
- [Analysis and discretization of the Ohta–Kawasaki equation]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}#pub21)
- [Structure-preserving approximation of the Cahn–Hilliard–Biot system]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}#pub15)

## Fractional, nonlocal, and stochastic equations

Fractional derivatives and nonlocal operators provide mathematical descriptions of
memory, anomalous diffusion, and long-range interaction. My work addresses
well-posedness, energy behaviour, order perturbations, stochastic forcing, and
computable approximations for such models.

Representative work:

- [A fractional de Rham complex for coframe-attached Maxwell equations]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}#pub24)
- [A nonlocal kinetic model for dilute polymers with anomalous diffusion]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}#pub22)
- [Time-fractional Cahn–Hilliard equation]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}#pub7)

## Control, coupled systems, and nonlinear acoustics

I investigate feedback stabilization and numerical control for nonlinear PDEs, as well
as coupled systems arising from flow, poroelasticity, transport, and wave propagation.
A recurring goal is to connect rigorous stability analysis with implementable algorithms.

Representative work:

- [Feedback stabilisation for the Cahn–Hilliard equation]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}#pub20)
- [Stabilization of nonisothermal Cahn–Hilliard trajectories]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}#pub18)
- [Nonlinear acoustics in velocity–enthalpy formulation]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}#pub17)

## Mathematical models in the life sciences

Phase-field, transport, tissue-mechanics, and vascular-network models make it possible
to study interacting biological processes across scales. Applications in my work include
tumor evolution, angiogenesis, immunotherapy, digit morphogenesis, and damage–repair
dynamics.

Representative work:

- [Time-fractional Fisher–KPP equation for tumor growth]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}#pub23)
- [Stochastic Cahn–Hilliard tumor-growth model]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}#pub16)
- [A 1D–0D–3D model for blood flow and transport in breast tissue]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}#pub8)
